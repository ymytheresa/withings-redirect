GLP-1 Tracker: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

GLP-1 Tracker is a personal app for tracking weight loss, body composition, food and medication. It has no accounts, no servers, no ads and no analytics.

Data stored on your device

Your profile, goals, weigh-ins, doses, meals, water, check-ins and settings are stored only on your iPhone. API keys and login tokens are kept in the iOS Keychain. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Apple Health

With your permission, the app reads body measurements, activity, sleep, nutrition and profile characteristics from Apple Health. It writes the meals and water you log back to Apple Health. Health data is never sent to the developer or any third party, and is never used for advertising.

Withings

If you connect your Withings account, the app downloads your own body-composition measurements directly from Withings to your iPhone. The connection uses Withings' OAuth login. This page's address only passes the one-time login code back to the app and stores nothing.

Food photos (Google Gemini)

When you analyze a meal photo, the photo and any note you add are sent to Google's Gemini API to estimate its nutrition. No name, account or health data is sent with it. Google's handling of that data is covered by the Gemini API terms. You can always log meals manually instead.

Not medical advice

The app's targets and analyses come from published guidelines and are for information only. Follow your doctor's instructions.
